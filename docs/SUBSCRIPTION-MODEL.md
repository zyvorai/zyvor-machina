# Machina: enterprise subscription

Machina is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Professional | Entry edition, updates and upgrades | Business hours, next-business-day critical response |
| Enterprise | Advanced edition, updates and upgrades | Priority business hours, four-business-hour critical response |
| Premium | Contracted enterprise scope and critical escalation | 24x7 critical intake, one-hour initial response target |

Priced per CPU socket per year: **USD 300 per socket per year worldwide (excl. tax)** or **INR 29,699 per socket per year in India (incl. 18% GST)**, with no minimum beyond one socket. Volume: 256-499 sockets -10%, 500-999 -15%, 1,000+ -20%; multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%; volume and multi-year together are capped at 25%. Minimums apply after discounts. Machina for Mac (the native client) is USD 40 per Mac per year worldwide or INR 3,999 in India; the team price is USD 34 / INR 3,399 per Mac with a minimum of 5 Macs (USD 169 / INR 16,995), by invoice.

Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev). Buy or get a quote: [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy). A 30-day trial key is available for production evaluation.

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
